  • D2 The request asked to map three supplier-quotation Form Fill responses into the same incident amount field. Provia does not allow mapping any value from a multi-response Form Fill into incident metadata, and mapping three single-response actions onto one shared field would let each submission silently overwrite the previous one, losing two of the three quotations. (resolved, Owner: Process owner)
    Each quotation gets its own case field (quote_1_amount, quote_2_amount, quote_3_amount) from its own single-response Form Fill action. A new Standard action, selecionar_proposta, is where a person compares the three and manually sets purchase_amount (and selected_supplier) once, with a mandatory comment. See forms.md for the full explanation and a documented alternative.
