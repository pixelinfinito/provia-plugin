  • Purchase request fields (not modeled as entity type; recommended disposition):
  • - Case metadata (workflow schema, needed for routing/reporting/approval thresholds): requester (native creator), department or cost center, request date (native createdAt), needed-by date, urgency/priority, supplier (entity reference to Supplier, once selected), estimated total amount, currency, budget code, purchase-order number (auto_number on the workflow once issued).
  • - Native workflow features, not duplicate custom fields: current approval decision and history (workflow action outcomes), overall request status (case/workflow status), assignee (action assignee).
  • - Form response only (collected at submission, mapped to the case only if a listed use needs it): line items (description, quantity, unit, estimated unit price), justification/business reason, delivery location, requested supplier quotations or attachments.
  • - Held in the Supplier entity, referenced rather than repeated on every request: supplier identity, contacts, payment terms, currency of billing, relationship status, account owner.
  • This disposition is a design recommendation for the next workflow/form design step; it is not workflow YAML or a form specification.

Functional coverage

  • Identity and classification: Included. Business identification (legal name, tax number) and procurement category support invoicing and routing.
  • Contacts and location: Included. Operational contact and address support quotation, ordering and delivery coordination.
  • Ownership and structure: Included. Account owner names who is accountable for the relationship.
  • Status and lifecycle: Included. Relationship status and start date support selection checks and periodic review.
  • Operational or commercial attributes: Included. Payment terms, billing currency and bank details are reused across purchase orders instead of being re-entered per request.
  • Relationships: Case. Purchase requests reference this Supplier record by entity selector; the request itself is not modeled as a second entity type.
  • Evidence and source: Pending. An evaluation/qualification record link is proposed only if the organization runs a defined supplier-evaluation process; not confirmed.

Setup and dependencies

  • Synthetic coverage example, not a mandatory model or confirmed policy; no source document was reviewed for this task.
  • Confirm owners, vocabularies (procurement category, payment terms), sources and access before configuring. No synchronization with an external vendor registry is configured.
  • Name is native. These fields do not duplicate UUID, creator or system timestamps.
  • Manual walkthrough: create 'Demonstration Supplier'; complete category, contact and payment terms before it can be selected on a purchase request or purchase order. Tax number and bank details can be completed later, before the first payment, if unknown at registration.
  • Configure supplier_code as auto_number, required false, no default. If the organization already assigns an external vendor code, keep this as text and drop the auto_number proposal to avoid a duplicate identifier.
  • bank_details holds sensitive financial data. Before collecting it, confirm field-level or role-based access in the destination; a sensitivity label alone does not restrict who can see the field.

Checks before process execution

  • Before selecting a supplier on a purchase request or issuing a purchase order, confirm identity, category, contact and relationship status. The status field does not itself block selection; that needs a separately configured check.
