# Angola

Use this reference for terminology and source selection, not as a statement of legal compliance. Source directory reviewed on 2026-09-10. Verify each relevant instrument again when using it for a decision.

## Working context

Use Portuguese appropriate to Angola unless another language is requested. Examples may use Luanda, AOA/Kz, Africa/Luanda, procurement teams and Recursos Humanos. A NIF may be relevant to a supplier process; collect it only if the process requires it. Do not substitute Brazilian identifiers or institutions.

Organization approval thresholds, cost-centre rules and service targets are policy inputs. Label synthetic amounts as illustrative company policy, never as statutory thresholds. Explain approval criteria to an authorized human Decision action; do not imply that a numeric metadata field automatically routes a workflow.

## Authoritative starting points

- Data protection: https://apd.ao/ao/legislacao/
- Labour-law instruments: https://www.lgt.gov.ao/documentos/
- Public administration, labour and social security: https://www.maptss.gov.ao/
- Tax administration: https://www.agt.minfin.gov.ao/PortalAGT/
- Finance legislation: https://www.minfin.gov.ao/legislacoes

These are starting points. Read the actual text and establish whether it is in force, amended, a proposal or repealed. Cross-border processing and sector-specific obligations need their own applicability check. Do not present a ministry landing page alone as proof of an obligation.

## Source record

For a legally relevant design decision, record country, authority, instrument title/number, article or section, publication/effective date, amendments or status checked, URL, verification date, and the specific implication for the process. When any of these cannot be established, state what remains unverified.

## Example boundary

“Finanças revê compras acima de 250.000 Kz” can be an explicitly supplied company rule. It must not be attributed to Angolan law without a verified instrument. An employee onboarding example is not a complete statement of labour-law obligations. Keep proposed HR data collection proportional to the actual task.
